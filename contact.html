Common questions

Frequently asked

Is the museum free to use?

Yes — completely. Egypt's Digital Museum is an educational project and always free to access.

Can I use images from the collection for my schoolwork?

Images sourced from public domain repositories may be used for non-commercial educational purposes. Please check each artifact page for specific licensing notes.

How do I suggest an artifact or correction?

Use the contact form above and select "Artifact inquiry" as the subject. We welcome contributions and corrections from enthusiasts and academics alike.

Is the virtual tour available on mobile?

The virtual exhibition is accessible on all devices. Individual era pages are fully optimised for mobile — the full 360° tour is in development.

What was this project built for?

Egypt's Digital Museum was developed for KCC ICT Day 2026 as an interactive educational platform connecting ancient history with modern web technology.
