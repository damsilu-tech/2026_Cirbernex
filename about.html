Why This Project?

A competition theme became a digital museum.

Egypt Digital Museum was created for the ICT Day Competition organised by Kakirawa Central College. Rather than treating the competition theme as simply a requirement to build a website, this project explores how modern web technology can transform the way people experience history and cultural heritage.

The result is an attempt to combine history, technology, interaction and visual storytelling into one digital museum experience.

Designed for Everyone

A digital museum should be accessible wherever people are.

The experience is designed to work across desktop, tablet and mobile — with careful attention to accessibility at every level.

📱

Responsive Design

Every page adapts to desktop, tablet and mobile screens — tested across a wide range of device sizes.

🌙

Light & Dark Themes

Choose between a bright editorial museum atmosphere and a darker immersive gallery experience. Preference is saved across pages.

⌨️

Keyboard Navigation

Every interactive element can be reached and activated using only a keyboard — no mouse required.

👁️

Visual Contrast

Text and interactive elements meet accessible contrast standards in both light and dark modes.

Meaningful HTML structure, aria labels, aria-live regions and focus management throughout the museum.

Research & Historical Responsibility

History presented with care.

Egypt Digital Museum is an educational digital experience — not a replacement for professional archaeological or historical institutions.

Historical information is presented responsibly and supported by reliable museum, archaeological and academic sources. Where scholarly debate exists — for example, around the exact dating of periods or the interpretation of artifacts — this is acknowledged rather than presented as settled fact.

The project aims to make Egyptian history easier to explore and understand, while respecting the complexity and cultural significance of Egypt's heritage.

📌
Source-based content

Historical claims are grounded in museum, academic and archaeological sources where possible.

🔍
Acknowledging uncertainty

Where dates and interpretations are debated, this is made clear to visitors.

🏛️
Cultural respect

Egypt's heritage is approached with educational respect — not as entertainment or novelty.

📖
Educational purpose

The museum is designed to make history more accessible — encouraging further exploration, not replacing scholarly resources.
