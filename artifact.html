Collections/Golden Mask

Mask of
Tutankhamun

An object that became a symbol of ancient Egypt.

The famous funerary mask of Tutankhamun was made for the burial of the young king. Its materials, imagery and craftsmanship reveal ideas about kingship, protection and the afterlife.

01READ THE OBJECT
More than decoration

Every detail
has a purpose.

The face presents the king in an idealized form. The striped headcloth, royal beard and protective imagery communicate identity and divine kingship.

Gold was especially meaningful in Egyptian religious thought. Its permanence and radiance made it a powerful material for an object intended to accompany a king into the afterlife.

01Royal identity

The mask presents Tutankhamun according to the visual language of kingship.

02Protection

Symbolic imagery helped protect the deceased on the journey beyond life.

03Transformation

The funerary context connects the king with regeneration and eternal life.
