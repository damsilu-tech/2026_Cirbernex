01 / Start here

Pictures that could
sound, mean & explain.

Ancient Egyptian writing used hundreds of signs representing sounds, objects and ideas. Hieroglyphs appeared on temples, tombs, statues, papyri and objects connected with daily and religious life.

Hieroglyphic writing was especially associated with monumental and sacred contexts. Egyptians also developed faster scripts, including hieratic and later demotic, for many practical uses.

09 / Famous inscriptions

Writing that
changed history.

Hieroglyphs survive in monuments, tombs, statues and stelae. Each inscription has a physical and historical context.

𓂀𓋹𓏏
ROSETTA STONE · PTOLEMAIC PERIOD

One text, three scripts.

The Rosetta Stone carries the same decree in hieroglyphic, demotic and Greek. Its parallel texts helped scholars unlock ancient Egyptian writing.

Historical context · 196 BCE
𓇳𓅃𓉢
TEMPLES · ROYAL & RELIGIOUS

Words carved for eternity.

Temple walls record royal names, rituals, offerings and stories. Monumental writing connected the king, gods and sacred space.

Context · architecture & ritual
𓂀𓆣𓋹
TOMBS · FUNERARY

Writing for the journey beyond.

Funerary texts and images formed part of a larger ritual environment, helping express beliefs about protection, identity and the afterlife.
